Privacy Policy

Short version: we collect nothing, and the app never touches the internet.

My App Spot does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

The app runs entirely on your Mac. It does not connect to the internet, contains no analytics or tracking, and sends no information to us or to any third party. Your list of applications, your searches, your favorites, and your settings never leave your device.

In-app purchases

The optional "Unlock Favorites" purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment details.

Your data rights

Because we collect no data, there is nothing on our side to access, correct, export, or delete. Anything the app stores (your settings and favorites) lives only in your macOS user account and is removed when you delete the app.

This website

This website (myappspot.app) uses Fathom Analytics, a privacy-friendly, cookieless service, to count visits. It sets no cookies and collects no personal data. This is separate from the app itself, which still collects nothing and makes no network connections.
